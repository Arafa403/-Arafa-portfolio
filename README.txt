CONTACT FORM SETUP
- The contact form is configured to send messages to arafamd7420@gmail.com using FormSubmit (https://formsubmit.co/).
- On the first submission, FormSubmit sends an activation/confirmation email to that mailbox. Open it and click the confirmation link before expecting future messages to arrive.
- The form needs an internet connection and will not send email when opened offline.
- FormSubmit is a third-party service; review its privacy terms before publishing.
